How it works

Start studying uses spaced repetition — it brings back words you find hard and spaces out ones you know. Answer each card: get it right and it comes back less often; miss it and it returns soon.

Modes

  • Converse — roleplay a dialogue; it speaks, you reply.
  • Quiz — quick multiple-choice.
  • Browse — every word, with audio and stroke order.

Buttons you'll see

  • hear it  ·  ½× hear it slowly
  • stroke order & writing practice (with a printable sheet)
  • say it and check your pronunciation (Chrome/Edge only)
  • reveal the pinyin hint

On desktop: press 1–4 to pick an answer, Space or Enter to continue.

The four tones

Mandarin is tonal: the same syllable said with a different pitch is a different word. The mark over the vowel tells you the tone — it matters as much as the letters. Tap the speaker to hear each.

Neutral tone — no mark, said light and quick: ma 吗 (turns a sentence into a question).

Same letters, different tone = different meaning: mǎi 买 (buy) vs mài 卖 (sell).
